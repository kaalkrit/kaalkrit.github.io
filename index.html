Resume CV

I am driven by a deep curiosity for the frontiers of theoretical physics. My passion lies in exploring the foundations and reconstruction of quantum theory, quantum field theory with a focus on axiomatization, unification methods, and mathematical physics.
Particularly, I am fascinated by the concept of a "physical theory of time." My ultimate goal is to understand the fundamental nature of mass, time, and charge. I seek to uncover the symmetries that connect the mathematical structures of physics to the laws governing our universe.
This quest leads me down the path of unification theories, where I hope to contribute to the ongoing effort to find a single, all-encompassing theory. Ultimately, my journey is one of continuous learning and exploration, fueled by the desire to unravel the mysteries of the cosmos.
