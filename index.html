In talking about my research experiences, In my previous research endeavours, I investigated the two-body problem-like situation in two consecutive semesters, simultaneously considering gravitational and electrostatic interactions. The studies are respectively submitted as preprints into the OSF and ArXiv, aiming to clarify the underlying motives of the existence of two types of electric charges and the role of gravitation in nature, heuristically. In another, asked, why nature prefers a system which is a heavier object at the centre of positive charge over another negatively charged body? I wrote a book chapter on quantum dynamics in Hindi Language to create resources for intended readers. In the winter of 2021, I delivered an online talk about unifications in mathematics. After the third year, I turned to the quantum foundation accidentally; It tried to talk about the mathematical transition from the classical picture to the quantum picture (quantization). I inquired about the Born-Jordan quantum condition to determine whether a classical complement form of this condition exists. Provided me with a better understanding of observables and the role of i in quantum mechanics as a linear operator. I was also inspired by self-inquiry. I composed brief articles that asked, Can one obtain the Klein Gordon wave equation without using the quantum operators? Should there be any distinction between the quantum wave equation and the governing law in the context of quantum mechanical formalisms? In principle, what are those fundamental quantities responsible for quantising the derived physical quantities?

It is a product of my attempt made in March 2020 to have a relativistic version of p2/2m. Whose application is potent enough to eliminate Klein’s Paradox and particle number violation and may finally establish a relativistic quantum wave equation and Pauli-Schr¨odinger’s equation. It has been solved for some ideal potential problems, Rel. Harmonic oscillator, finding eigenvalues of quartic-perturbed harmonic. Concurrently, empirically test to obtain Lorentz covariant form and limits, reduce and compare the expressions to reported forms (A.L. Harvey,(1972)) and also aiming to apply the relation in classical relativistic theory as well, working under the supervision of Dr VK Ojha in NIT Surat. I am motivated to pursue Quantum Field Theory (QFT) research by my interest in certain open theoretical challenges in physics-mathematics conjunctions. I am personally drawn to the axiomatic approach to field theory because it seems like a natural extension of the quantum foundation, which I have been extensively studying and working on since my third year in university, as reflected by my last few UG research projects and partly in the master’s dissertation. I recall the motivation of Hilbert’s 6th problem (1900): On making an endeavor axiomatically for physical science in which mathematics plays an important role. In my understanding, the axiomatic approach to quantum field theory is necessary to be done. I believe that a PhD in theoretical physics will provide me with the necessary skills and knowledge to make appropriate contributions to these fields, and I am still a passionate learner. When I first learned about quantum field theory (2023), I was captivated by its elegance, power, and difficulties. However, QFT provides a unified framework. My interest in the axiomatic approach evolved around (August 2023) and seeks to provide a rigorous mathematical foundation for QFT and thus provide a better and broader view. Axiomatic QFT is particularly useful for studying non-perturbative aspects of the theory. I also believe that the axiomatic approach to QFT is essential for developing a deeper understanding of this fundamental theory.
