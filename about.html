A Life Entangled with Physics: A Passion for Knowledge and Beauty


My intrigue with physics began in middle school, where I discovered the captivating dance between abstract mathematics and the phenomena that shape our world. This fascination deepened in high school. The elegance of Noether's theorems, revealing the profound connection between continuous symmetry and conservation laws, fueled my desire to pursue physics. I yearned to understand the profound beauty revealed through generalization, the "bird's-eye view" of nature's compactification. But a question lingered: how to express the very essence of physics? Driven by this inquiry, I delved into understanding the fundamental properties of mass, time, and charge. My journey wasn't solely mathematical; I explored physics through the lens of language, composing poems to grapple with its complexities. This practice instilled in me a deep appreciation for the seriousness and weight of the scientific pursuit. Naturally, I gravitated towards theoretical physics, drawn to its intellectual elegance. My undergraduate studies in Physics (2019-2024) at the Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT) in Surat, India, provided a robust foundation. Formal education unveiled the multifaceted nature of our physical world: lazy (least action), discrete (quantum), probabilistic (uncertainty principle), directional (parity violation), yet ultimately finite (cut-offs). This exposure ignited a fire within me – a yearning for a comprehensive picture. College also fostered my inclination towards research. College life also instilled in me the value of patience, meticulousness, and openness to exploration, all while adhering to defined syllabi.
Originality and the pursuit of truth became my guiding principles, with my emotions and intuition serving as valuable tools for navigating challenges. My research endeavors have been diverse. My passion for physics extends beyond the established framework. I am deeply interested in Quantum Field Theory (QFT) and its potential to address open theoretical challenges arising from the intricate interplay of physics and mathematics. The axiomatic approach to QFT particularly resonates with me as a natural extension of my exploration of the quantum foundation, evident in my recent undergraduate research projects and partially in my Master's dissertation. This approach aligns with the spirit of Hilbert's 6th problem (1900), emphasizing the need for an axiomatic framework in physical sciences, a necessity for a deeper understanding of QFT. A Ph.D. in theoretical physics would equip me with the necessary skills and knowledge to contribute significantly to these fields. My journey began in 2023 with the captivating elegance and complexity of QFT. However, its potential to provide a unified theoretical framework fuels my passion.
